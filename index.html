Groove Skater

A tiny skater is the needle of a spinning record. Where it skates is what you hear: skate with the spin and the song plays backwards, jump and the needle lifts. Press PLAY and the motor cuts out, so you keep the song going by pushing on the beat.

Canvas and Web Audio. The original animation measured frame by frame, beats found in the browser so your own songs work too.
