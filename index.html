Orot

A slow photo journal. One photo and a few words a day, and every 100 entries become a chapter. No feed, no likes, no account: the journal stays on your phone, and you share a moment only when you choose. Version 2.0 puts each moment on a map of where it happened.

React Native and TypeScript, designed, built and shipped solo. Entries live in a local SQLite database behind a PIN or biometric lock, share cards come in 7 frame styles, and releases go out to both stores through fastlane.

Moment Select

Swipe through hundreds of photos and keep the ones you want. Made for the day the studio sends you 1,500 wedding shots and asks you to pick 30. The best shots come first, near-identical bursts are grouped, and two people can pick separately and merge.

Everything runs in the browser: face detection, sharpness and exposure scores, and a small model that learns from your picks and reorders the rest. Nothing is uploaded, and it works offline.

Groove Skater

A tiny skater is the needle of a spinning record. Where it skates is what you hear: skate with the spin and the song plays backwards, jump and the needle lifts. Press PLAY and the motor cuts out, so you keep the song going by pushing on the beat.

Canvas and Web Audio. The original animation measured frame by frame, beats found in the browser so your own songs work too.
