# simplefunction

Danny's page: small things you can play with, mostly on phones.

- [Orot](https://getorot.app): a slow photo journal for iOS and Android. One photo a day, and every 100 entries become a chapter. Private by default.
- [Moment Select](https://simplefunction-lab.github.io/moment-select/): swipe through hundreds of photos and keep the ones you want, analyzed on your device and never uploaded.
- [Groove Skater](https://groove-skater.vercel.app/): a tiny skater is the needle of a spinning record. An interactive tribute to "Roller" by Bénédict Aubry, made with his permission.

Plain HTML, served by GitHub Pages. To add a project: put its preview in `projects/<name>/` and add an `<article class="project">` to `index.html`.
